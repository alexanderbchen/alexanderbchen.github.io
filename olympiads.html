2.

Building one — SSAMO

Then I started building contests for other people. I founded SSAMO, the Shady Side Academy Mathematics Olympiad, and I’m president of the math club and captain of the ARML team. The idea was simple: the competitions that shaped me should exist for the students coming up behind me, at our own school.

I write the problems myself, not just host them: a monthly Problem of the Month, this fall a geometry set, and a full 25-problem mock AMC 10, every problem verified in Python and solutions included.
